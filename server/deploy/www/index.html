Sebastian's Bike Display

Sebastian's Bike Display is a simple bike computer doing what I need:

  • Showing power from a BLE power sensor.
  • Showing cadence from a BLE power sensor.
  • Showing Heart Rate from a BLE HRM.
  • Showing L/R balance.
  • Showing speed, distance, duration, climb from the phone's GNSS positions.
  • Uploading the ride to Strava, chosing the right bike.

The app is not installable from Google Play.
